ABOUT THE PROGRAMME
Learn by creating real projects
Future Skills Academy helps learners move from basic computer knowledge to confident digital creation. Every lesson combines a clear concept with guided practice, personal feedback, and a useful project result.
You do not need previous professional experience. You only need curiosity, regular practice, and the willingness to improve your work one step at a time.
WHAT YOU WILL PRACTISE
A focused learning path
  • Build clear page structure with HTML

  • Style readable and attractive pages with CSS

  • Create practical projects for your portfolio

  • Review your work with mouse and keyboard
Start small, practise consistently, and let every project show your progress.
